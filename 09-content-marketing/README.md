# Content Marketing

Content marketing is the process of creating and sharing valuable, relevant, and consistent content to attract and retain a target audience. Instead of directly promoting products or services, content marketing focuses on educating, informing, and solving problems for potential customers.

Good content builds trust, improves SEO, increases website traffic, and supports other digital marketing channels such as social media and email marketing.
