# Email Marketing

Email marketing is one of the most effective digital marketing channels for building relationships with customers, promoting products or services, and increasing conversions.

Unlike social media platforms, where algorithms determine who sees your content, email allows you to communicate directly with your audience.

This section introduces the fundamentals of email marketing, explains common terminology, and demonstrates how to create effective email campaigns.

---

# What You'll Learn

By the end of this section, you should be able to:

- Understand the purpose of email marketing
- Build an email list responsibly
- Create newsletters and promotional emails
- Understand lead magnets
- Measure campaign performance
- Improve open rates and click-through rates
- Follow email marketing best practices
