# Social Media Marketing

Social Media Marketing (SMM) is the process of using social media platforms to promote a brand, engage with an audience, drive website traffic, and achieve business goals. It combines content creation, audience engagement, and performance analysis to build a strong online presence.

This section introduces the fundamentals of social media marketing, explains how major platforms are used for business, and outlines strategies for creating effective content and growing an audience organically.

---

# What You'll Learn

By the end of this section, you should be able to:
