# Foundations of Digital Marketing

The term "digital marketing" refers to the promotion of goods, services or brands using digital devices like the search engines, websites, social media sites, e-mail and online ads. Digital marketing skills have become essential nowadays for any company, since they make it possible to target audiences, measure results and constantly improve marketing techniques.

In this section we will consider the key concepts which constitute the base of digital marketing. No matter whether you want to become a marketer, launch your own business, improve websites or learn how marketing works online – you need to know these fundamentals to proceed to SEO, WordPress, advertising platforms and analytics.

---

## Objectives

Upon completion of this section, you will be able to:

- Define digital marketing and explain its significance;
- Differentiate between offline and online marketing;
- Describe the customer journey;
- Explain the concept of marketing funnel and its stages;
- Create a buyer persona;
- Describe the AIDA model;
- List major channels of digital marketing;
- Explain how various marketing strategies complement each other and help in achieving business goals.
