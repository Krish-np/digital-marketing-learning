# 1. Introduction to Digital Marketing

Digital marketing is a promotion of products, services, or a brand through internet and connected technologies.

It is different from traditional marketing because businesses can target specific audiences, analyze their campaigns, and improve their marketing strategies based on data.

Examples of digital marketing include:

- Getting into Google Search results
- Posting advertisements on Facebook or Instagram
- Sending out emails
- Blogging
- Producing video content
- Publishing on LinkedIn
- Optimization for search engines

Digital marketing is utilized by firms of any sizes – from local restaurants to big multinational corporations.

---

## The Objectives of Digital Marketing

Main objectives that are usually pursued when using digital marketing may include the following:

- Brand Awareness Increase
- Generation of Leads
- Website Traffic Growth
- Increasing Online Sales
- Building Relationships With Customers
- Retention of Customers
- Improving Brand Credibility

# 4. Marketing Funnel

Marketing funnel shows all the stages which people usually pass before becoming a client.

```text
Awareness
    ↓
Interest
    ↓
Consideration
    ↓
Conversion
    ↓
Retention
    ↓
Advocacy
```

### Awareness

Potential customers find out about your company via search engines, ads, referrals, or social media.

### Interest

People start to learn about the products/services offered by the company.

### Consideration

Clients start comparing your business with competitors.

### Conversion

Desired actions are completed, such as:

- Product/service purchase
- Contact form completion
- Appointment booking
- Newsletter subscription

### Retention

Businesses continue offering value after the purchase in order to gain repeat clients.

### Advocacy

Happy customers promote your business via referrals or social media.

# 5. Customer Journey

Customer journey includes all customer-business interactions that occur prior to and after the purchase.

Example:

```text

        ↓
Visit Website
        ↓
Read Blog
        ↓
Browse Products
        ↓
Contact Business
        ↓
Purchase
        ↓
Receive Follow-Up Email
        ↓
```

Each of those interactions affects the image that the customer creates of the company.

It enables marketers to find ways to enhance customer experiences.

# 7. AIDA Model

AIDA marketing model is one of the most popular and well-known models.

```text
Attention
      ↓
Interest
      ↓
Desire
      ↓
Action
```

### Attention

Get the attention of your audience using headlines, visuals, ads etc.

### Interest

Offer some valuable information which will make the audience interested to learn more.

### Desire

Show the value that you can offer showing how you can solve their problems.

### Action

Make users take action such as buying your products or subscribing to your services.

# 9. How Everything Works Together

A simple example illustrates how these concepts connect:

A local bakery writes an SEO-friendly article about birthday cakes.

They find the page when they are searching on Google.

How they get in: The visitor searches the web and then finds the bakery and follows the bakery to its Instagram page.

One week later, the bakery offers a seasonal promotion via social media.

The visitor orders.

Following delivery, a thank-you email is sent from the bakery with a request for a review.

This journey involves:

- SEO

- Content Marketing

- Social Media Marketing

- Email Marketing

- Customer Retention

All channel support each other but they are not independent.

# 10. Common Misconceptions

Digital marketing is just social media.

Social media is just one aspect of digital marketing.

---

### "SEO delivers immediate results."

SEO is a long term strategy that can take up to several months to see consistent results.

---

More visitors to the website equals more sales.

Traffic by itself isn't enough. Visitors that are relevant and a good user experience are key.

---

### "Paid advertising replaces SEO."

Paid advertising delivers instant exposure; SEO delivers long-term organic traffic. Most companies combine both.
